Institutional Compliance Checklist

  • SCOPE
    Data locality: Preprocessing is centralized and the four clients are randomized shards; this prototype does not demonstrate institution-local data residency.
  • SCOPE
    Resampling: The canonical manifest uses none. Optional SMOTE-Tomek runs are client-training-only; their reported epsilon is nominal, not formal DP for original records.
  • LIMITED
    Cryptographic auditing: The Groth16 circuit checks a weighted relation and Poseidon commitment for 33 quantized output-layer values; it does not prove training correctness or model accuracy.
