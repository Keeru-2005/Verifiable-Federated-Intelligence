\begin{abstract}
This paper draft records the current VFI proof-of-concept implementation for internal review and is not submission-ready. The prototype connects chronological transaction preprocessing, Flower training across four simulated clients, client-side DP-SGD, a Groth16 proof of a limited output-layer aggregation relation, and smart-contract audit logging. The dataset is split chronologically into 763,121 training, 109,017 validation, and 218,034 test rows, with 1,226, 217, and 302 positive labels. Feature transforms are fitted on pooled training rows before those rows are randomized into client shards; the clients do not represent independent financial institutions. In the validation ablation, client-local SMOTE-Tomek achieved F1 0.7511 and average precision 0.8012 at the server-selected round 8, but its one-time test attempt is invalid due to a checkpoint/threshold mismatch and is excluded from final-test claims. The valid BCE model with positive weight 25 reports validation F1 0.7163 and final-test F1 0.6791, with final-test average precision 0.7274. The test period has been consulted during project iteration and is not an untouched publication estimate. The circuit proves only a sample-count-weighted arithmetic relation for 33 quantized output-layer parameters and their Poseidon commitment; it does not prove complete model training, accuracy, or metric truthfulness.
\end{abstract}

\section{Current Pipeline}
The canonical manifest identifies a chronological whole-timestamp split of 1,090,172 rows: 763,121 training (1,226 positive), 109,017 validation (217 positive), and 218,034 test rows (302 positive). Causal transaction-history features follow the split. StandardScaler, PCA, and the post-PCA scaler are fit on pooled training rows. The train-only projection is shared across four randomized training shards to provide compatible feature coordinates; it is not institution-local preprocessing.

The classifier is a PyTorch MLP over 32 PCA-transformed features, not a GNN doing message passing in the classification loop. Its final layer remains \texttt{Linear(32,1)}, with 32 weights and one bias. The client uses Opacus DP-SGD, while Flower aggregates using FedAvg. The formal-DP candidate reports $\epsilon=7.75214$ at $\delta=10^{-5}$ and RNG mode \texttt{standard\_prng\_demo\_only}, which is not production-secure randomness.

\section{Cryptographic Scope}
The Circom circuit constrains a sample-count-weighted arithmetic relation for 33 quantized output-layer parameters and their Poseidon commitment. The Solidity contract verifies the proof and records round metadata. The circuit does not prove full-model training, correctness of local training, source data integrity, model accuracy, or metric truthfulness. Hashes and metrics are not all circuit-constrained.

\section{Limitations}
Clients are simulated randomized shards, and preprocessing is centralized. The data retriever can use mock fallback data, so source provenance and checksum must be retained for reported experiments. DP RNG is demonstrative rather than production-secure. The SMOTE-Tomek accounting does not establish a formal privacy guarantee for original records. A fresh, preregistered chronological holdout and real institution-aware partitions would be needed to support stronger deployment or generalization claims.
